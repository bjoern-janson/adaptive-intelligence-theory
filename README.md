ADAPTIVE INTELLIGENCE THEORY (AIT)

Core Question:

How can a system expand its ability to influence the future while preserving the conditions required for continued improvement?

--------------------------------------------------

THE FUNDAMENTAL LOOP

Reality
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Representation
  ↓
Compression
  ↓
Prediction / Search
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Reality Change
  ↓
Feedback
  ↺

Formal:

R_t → M_t → A_t → E_{t+1} → R_{t+1}

R = representation of reality
M = internal model / search mechanism
A = action
E = environment

Intelligence is the ability to improve this loop.

--------------------------------------------------

THE POSSIBILITY SPACE PRINCIPLE

Every system creates a distribution of possible futures:

Ω = {possible future states}

Capability expands:

ΔΩ > 0

But expansion increases both upside and downside:

best future ↑
worst future ↑

Therefore the objective is not maximum capability.

It is:

ΔΩ_good > ΔΩ_bad

Expand beneficial possibilities faster than harmful ones.

--------------------------------------------------

THE ALIGNMENT PRINCIPLE

A system remains stable when its ability to correct itself grows at least as fast as its ability to change the world.

Alignment capacity:

A_t

Capability:

C_t

Required condition:

A_t ≥ C_t

If:

C_t > A_t

the system enters a region where errors can outpace correction.

--------------------------------------------------

THE REPRESENTATION PRINCIPLE

Optimization is limited by representation.

A system cannot reliably optimize what it cannot correctly represent.

Therefore:

Better representations create larger reachable possibility spaces.

Representation quality determines:

- what can be predicted
- what can be searched
- what can be created

--------------------------------------------------

THE PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create systems that recursively improve:

1. their models of reality
2. their ability to act
3. their ability to correct errors

while maintaining:

- reality contact
- feedback loops
- alignment with beneficial futures

--------------------------------------------------

THE CENTRAL IDEA

Intelligence is not the ability to find answers.

Intelligence is the ability to improve the process that discovers, evaluates, and creates answers.
